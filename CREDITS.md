## Photography Replacement Guide

To replace any photo:

1. Find a suitable photo on [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) or [Pexels](https://pexels.com).
2. Copy the photo ID (the string after `/photo-` in the URL).
3. Use the format: `https://images.unsplash.com/photo-{ID}?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1920&q=85`
4. Add the photographer credit to the table above.
5. Keep `?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1920&q=85` parameters for consistent delivery.

For Pexels: `https://images.pexels.com/photos/{ID}/pexels-photo-{ID}.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&w=1920`
